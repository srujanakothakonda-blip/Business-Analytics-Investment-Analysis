# Business Analytics – Investment Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes investment preferences and financial behavior using a dataset of 40 participants. The analysis explores demographic distribution, investment choices, savings objectives, investment duration, expected returns, and sources of financial information.

## Objectives

The project covers the following 10 Business Analytics tasks:

1. Data Overview
2. Gender Distribution
3. Descriptive Statistics
4. Most Preferred Investment Avenue
5. Reasons for Investment
6. Savings Objectives
7. Common Information Sources
8. Investment Duration
9. Expectations from Investments
10. Correlation Analysis

## Tools & Skills

- Microsoft Excel
- Business Analytics
- Data Analysis
- Descriptive Statistics
- Data Visualization
- Correlation Analysis
- Data Interpretation

## Key Findings

- The dataset contains 40 participant records.
- 25 participants are male and 15 are female.
- Mutual Funds are the most preferred investment avenue, selected by 18 participants.
- Retirement Planning is the most common savings objective, reported by 24 participants.
- Financial Consultants are the most commonly reported source of investment information, with 16 participants.
- The most common expected\-return range is 20%–30%, reported by 32 participants.
- Correlation analysis was performed among age, investment duration, and expected return.

## Analysis Performed

The project includes frequency analysis, descriptive statistics, investment preference analysis, savings behavior analysis, expected\-return analysis, and correlation analysis. Charts and visualizations were created to communicate the results clearly.

## Conclusion

The analysis provides insight into the investment behavior and preferences represented in the dataset. Mutual Funds emerged as the most frequently selected investment avenue, while Retirement Planning was the most frequently reported savings objective. The project demonstrates practical application of data analysis, descriptive statistics, visualization, and interpretation techniques.
